Popular topics

Food

Is eating pork haram?

Yes. The Quran prohibits eating pork, except in a situation of necessity when someone has no other option to survive.

Evidence: Quran 2:173 and 5:3.

Lifestyle

Is drinking alcohol haram?

Yes. The Quran tells believers to avoid intoxicants.

Evidence: Quran 5:90.

Worship

Is prayer important in Islam?

Yes. Establishing prayer is a central obligation in Islam.

Evidence: Quran 2:43.

Character

Is lying allowed?

Islam teaches truthfulness and warns against false speech. Specific situations can have different rulings.

Evidence: Quran 9:119.

Money

What is riba?

Riba refers to prohibited forms of increase or interest in financial dealings. Specific modern financial products need individual scholarly review.

Evidence: Quran 2:275.
